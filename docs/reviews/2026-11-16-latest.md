## R8 — High: a fresh manual plan trusts an assessment from an older policy

Locations: `internal/action/plan.go:198–206`,
`internal/app/actions.go:105–130,230–253`.

The planner only requires `Assessment.Status == qualified`. It does not
compare `Assessment.PolicyRevision` with the current effective diagnostic
settings. A new CLI process can load a stricter policy and immediately
execute a diagnosis produced under the old one. The coordinator's policy
gate compares the new proposal against the new configuration, so it agrees;
fresh live reads confirm the transfer is unchanged but cannot establish
that the longer evidence window has elapsed. SPEC §§5, 6 and 10 require the
current policy and renewed eligibility to govern manual writes too.

Reproduced with the existing deterministic application harness: qualify a
public transfer over three hours while paused; create a new Service over
the same state with `no_progress_window = "24h"`; preview is eligible, and
Act sends `manager.queue.fail` and `client.stop` through a matching test
executor, returning succeeded. No new engine cycle occurs, matching the
headless CLI path after a configuration edit. Both assertions fail in
`TestReviewChangedTimingBeforeManualAction`.

Require an assessment made under the current effective **settings** revision
(`Assessment.PolicyRevision` is `Settings.Revision()`, not the broader
`Effective.Revision`), or recompute from retained observations under that
policy before allowing the action. Missing/legacy revisions must fail
closed or be recomputed. Explain the need for reevaluation without resolving
the finding. Cover longer windows, availability requirements and scoped
policy changes; do not force requalification merely for a mode-only change.
Keep diagnostic identity separate from operational permission identity.

## R9 — Medium: stale client facts can establish replacement completion

Locations: `internal/engine/outcomes.go:51–60,113–125`.

Outcome advancement requires a fresh manager queue, but reads the associated
client's cached Transfer without requiring a successful current client read
or checking its evidence time. `Present` remains true when that client is
offline. A newly observed replacement queue row can therefore be paired
with pre-recovery completion facts and reported as verified completion now.
This contradicts SPEC §§3.2 and 7 step 6 and the intended evidence boundary.

Reproduced: observe replacement hash B complete at t0, create an outcome for
old hash A at t0+1m, then make the client fail and introduce a fresh manager
row for B. At t0+11m the outcome becomes completed, with CompletedAt set to
that cycle despite no successful post-recovery client observation.
`TestReviewOutcomeMustUseFreshClient` fails.

Keep fresh manager evidence of a grab separate from fresh client evidence
of progress/completion. Pass client freshness into tracking (including
complete listings and backoff), and require suitable post-grab evidence
before initializing/comparing its progress baseline. Test failed, incomplete
and backed-off client reads while manager reads remain healthy. Preserve
the last known state with visible freshness rather than inventing failure.

## R10 — Medium: completing the download freezes an unsettled search forever

Locations: `internal/store/outcomes.go:160–174`,
`internal/engine/outcomes.go:39–62`.

Both OpenOutcomes and ManagersAwaitingSearch exclude completed/untracked
download outcomes irrespective of search state. If the download completes
while its search is queued/running/unobserved, the command is never read or
expired again. Activity can permanently report an accepted/running search.
SPEC §§7 and 9 distinguish these outcomes independently.

Reproduced: queued EpisodeSearch #77 and a complete replacement are seen in
one cycle; the outcome becomes completed and search accepted. Change #77
to completed and run another healthy cycle: search stays accepted.
`TestReviewSearchSurvivesDownloadCompletion` fails.

Select work while either outcome still needs observation. Stop advancing
terminal download state while continuing/expiring its search under the
existing bounded window. Cover completed and tracking-expired downloads,
command-list outages, restart, and no further command polling once both
outcomes are terminal. Do not replay acquisition requests.

| Documentation/checkpoint only | Links, wording, diff check | No Go suite or real-instance rerun solely to make evidence point to HEAD |
